Connect Students and Teachers Directly

Apex Tuitions is a platform designed to help students, parents and teachers connect directly for tuition. Students can discover teachers according to their subjects, classes, boards, teaching mode and learning requirements. Teachers can create their profiles and connect with students looking for tuition.

Online and Home Tuition

Find teachers for online tuition or home tuition based on your learning needs. Apex Tuitions supports direct teacher-student communication so that tuition arrangements can be discussed directly between the parties.

School Subjects, Skills and Competitive Exams

Explore teachers for school and college subjects, CBSE, ICSE, State Boards, competitive examinations, languages, music, art, public speaking, creative skills and other learning requirements.

Zero Middlemen & Commissions

Commercial aggregators cut 30% to 50% from teachers and burden parents. Apex connects families directly on WhatsApp with zero cuts.

Verified Mentors

Every educator profile is checked across school credentials, degree qualifications, and subject specializations.

Inclusive & Flexible

Connecting across school curriculums (CBSE, ICSE, State Boards), competitive milestones, and creative arts across global time zones.

Core Charter & Principles

  • Direct Autonomy: Educators set their own fair hourly/monthly terms and retain 100% of their compensation directly from families.
  • Zero-Commission Access: Free direct connections for students and parents, eliminating agency commission fees.
  • Mutual Confirmation: Both educators and students mutually confirm schedules in their portal to maintain genuine community accountability.
