Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Apex Online Tuitions collects, uses and handles information when you use the platform.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Apex, we may collect account information such as name, email address and login-related information; teacher profile information such as qualification, experience, subjects, teaching mode, location and fee preferences; and student/parent information such as student name, class, board, subjects, location and preferred timings.

We may also receive technical information such as browser/device information, approximate usage information, IP-related security information and logs needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts and profiles.
  • To help teachers and students discover and contact each other.
  • To facilitate direct communication, including links to WhatsApp where enabled.
  • To provide, maintain, secure and improve the platform.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access and security incidents.
  • To communicate important account, service and policy updates.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Profile Visibility and Direct Contact

Information included in a teacher or student profile may be visible to other users for the purpose of finding suitable educational connections. If you choose to share a WhatsApp number or other contact details, those details may be used by another user to contact you directly. Users should share only information they are comfortable making available for this purpose.

4. WhatsApp and Third Parties

When you use WhatsApp or another external service to communicate, that service processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Apex does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

5. Service Providers

We may use trusted technology providers for hosting, databases, authentication, analytics, security, communications and payments. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services and subject to their applicable terms and safeguards.

6. Legal Disclosure

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, lawful government requests, protect users or the public, investigate suspected fraud or abuse, or protect the rights and security of Apex and its users.

7. We Do Not Sell Personal Information Simply to Sell It

Apex does not intend to sell users’ personal information as a standalone commercial product. Information may nevertheless be processed or shared with service providers as necessary to operate the platform, provide requested services, protect the service, or comply with law.

8. Payments

If paid platform or membership services are introduced, payment information may be processed by the relevant payment provider. Apex should not require users to send payment-card details through ordinary profile or WhatsApp messages.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website and its service providers may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve functionality and support security. You can manage certain browser settings, although disabling some technologies may affect functionality.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect passwords and avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily.

11. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, legitimate business and educational-platform purposes, security, dispute handling, legal compliance and other purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable law.

12. Children and Minors

Apex may contain profiles relating to school-age learners. Parents or guardians should supervise minors’ use of the platform and communications with teachers. Users should not post unnecessary sensitive information about children.

13. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, updating or deletion of certain personal information, as well as choices concerning communications. Requests may be sent to the contact address below. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and record-keeping purposes.

14. International Processing

Some technology providers used by Apex may process information in locations outside India. Where applicable, such processing will be subject to the provider’s terms, contractual safeguards and applicable legal requirements.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our platform, practices or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published here with a revised date.
